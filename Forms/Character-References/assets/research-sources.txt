Andrew Blake-Newton (Will Saville) References
Housing Choice Voucher research sources
Reviewed September 23, 2026

1. HUD Housing Choice Voucher tenant guidance
https://www.hud.gov/topics/housing_choice_voucher_program_section_8
Topics: waiting lists, tenant rent portion, payment standards, HAP, portability, tenant responsibilities.

2. HUD HCV landlord resources
https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/housing-choice-vouchers-landlord
Topics: landlord benefits, timely payments, financial protection, inspections, HCV program structure.

3. HUD HCV landlord forms and HAP contract
https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/housing-choice-vouchers-landlord-forms
Topics: HAP contract and monthly housing assistance payment.

4. HUD HCV Myth-Busting and Benefits Fact Sheet
https://files.hudexchange.info/resources/documents/PIH-HCV-Landlord-Myth-Busting-and-Benefits-Fact-Sheet.pdf
Topics: HCV tenants as typically long-term tenants, 7 to 8 years average in a unit, no documented greater unit damage or nonpayment compared with non-HCV tenants.

5. MSHDA Housing Choice Voucher landlord guidance
https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/rental/hcv-landlords/housing-choice-voucher-landlords
Topics: screening, rent reasonableness, affordability, tenant portion, HAP contract, tenant and owner responsibilities.

6. MSHDA HCV landlord payment information
https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/rental/hcv-landlords/housing-choice-vouchers-landlords---payment-information
Topics: HAP processing on or before the fifth business day and direct deposit.

7. MSHDA HCV waiting-list information
https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/rental/housing-choice-voucher/mshda-housing-choice-voucher-hcv-waiting-list-information
Topics: open and closed waiting lists, application order, preferences, and applicant updates.

8. MSHDA Fair Housing
https://www.michigan.gov/mshda/fairhousing
Topics: Michigan source-of-income protection, vouchers, and small-landlord exemption.

9. Michigan Legislature, Public Act 178 of 2024 source-of-income language
https://www.legislature.mi.gov/documents/2023-2024/publicact/htm/2024-PA-0178.htm
Topic: when a landlord uses a minimum income threshold, a rent voucher or subsidy is subtracted from monthly rent before evaluating the applicant's income against the threshold.

10. Center on Budget and Policy Priorities analysis of HUD data
https://www.cbpp.org/research/housing/families-wait-years-for-housing-vouchers-due-to-inadequate-funding
Topic: families who ultimately received vouchers waited close to two and a half years nationally on average in the analyzed data; some large housing agencies had average waits up to eight years.

11. Public reporting supplied for the July 2023 attack
https://medium.com/prismnpen/michigan-trans-man-shot-in-his-wheelchair-called-tr-nny-f-ggot-ad4bb52ab59

Notes
- Program facts are general guidance and do not replace an individual PHA rent calculation or legal advice.
- The project distinguishes personal income from the separate HAP payment made to the landlord.
- The project does not claim that voucher holders are guaranteed to remain in one unit or are statistically less likely to abandon a lease than all non-voucher renters. Instead, it uses HUD's documented long-term-tenancy data and program compliance requirements.
