# Lanternweald — Tenth-Pass Living World and Narrative Continuity

**Established November 5, 2026.** Supplements the original living world bible and nine prior passes; it changes no published ending. The Lanternweald is a separate original place of seven districts connected by consensual Threadways, not a copy of Dreamlight Valley. Its cozy quality comes from recognizable neighbors, seasonal routines, small institutions, household repairs, repeated invitations and shared consequences.

## Seven districts as a place where people continue living

### Lanternwell Square: the daily board

At dawn, Mara posts bakery prices and collects payment for wholesale flour. A separate maintenance notice shows safe approaches to the well, not private addresses. Neri keeps the arrival register, now with a **public service layer** (opening times, closures, weather warnings) and a **restricted safety layer** (only voluntarily supplied emergency contacts, correctable through a discreet process). The bakery's chalkboard is not an index of romances. Edda can ask for the notices to be placed where they can actually be read and reached. By evening, old notices are removed and late arrivals can ask a registrar rather than follow another visitor home. The same characters have work, needs and boundaries whether Jasper comes through today or not.

### Tideglass Cove: tide boards and the cost of repair

Fen measures silt, tide and the surface overflow after storms; the public board records **conditions under which a particular test passed**, never a blanket promise that the water is safe. Debris screens need cleaning, named duty workers need relief, and winter ice can change the pattern. The historic sealed drainage passage is not the same thing as the accessible upper maintenance hatch or the temporary surface channel. Sela monitors garden soil near runoff; Lio owns the skiff's repair decisions; Tamsin determines what work requires a qualified builder. Maui's showmanship can brighten a hard day, but it must not replace Fen's warnings or anybody's pay.

### Jasmine Steps: exchange, water, and an unhurried seat

The vale's own tea-serving steps host seasonal visitors, small repairs, and trades that depend on the rainwater system. A kettle borrowed from Lanternwell has a return date. A shopkeeper may refuse a customer or take an evening off. **Do not confuse this district with Iroh's Jasmine Dragon in Ba Sing Se.** Iroh can arrive by invitation, but his staff Mina, his neighbor Lian, and his homeworld's shop accounts do not magically migrate to the vale.

### Riverstone Terrace: gardens and difficult conversations

Gardeners track drought, spring runoff and winter storage. Residents can petition to change where water flows and whose path a detour interrupts. Edda's experience of a route counts as primary evidence, not a charming anecdote. Katara can share ideas from the Southern clinic without taking charge of the vale's health system. Aang may design a kite, Toph may test a footing, and neither action allows them to override a resident whose home is at risk.

### Moonroot Archive: the right to leave a blank

Neri accepts corrections to public ledgers without publishing confidential testimony. Orin's historical method distinguishes verified witnesses, interpretation, missing records, and protected identities. Severus can challenge a flattering or cruel history without controlling the final public narrative. The erased keeper signature in the Closed Bells record stays **unknown**. A privately corrected ferry time is not proof of who removed that name. The archive lends copies, not the right to someone's memories or grief.

### Brasswatch Yard: skilled work needs wages

The public workshop has a separate civilian safety procedure, a paid roster, a tool-return shelf and an accessible exit. Renn and Aster have schedules and lives outside emergency work. A worker can question a shift or file a safety complaint without having to seek approval from Danse, especially if Danse is part of the problem. Danse and Toph bring different ways of finding structural weaknesses; measurements must specify weather conditions and limitations. An unsafe storage room stays closed until the replacement passes inspection.

### Starwake Pier: music isn't free labor

Instrument loans, rehearsal hours, stage capacity, wind closures, repair budgets and volunteer rest all matter. Greg may offer an original performance when he wants; his choice is not a standing obligation. Tamsin can close the west side even during a beloved festival. The wind-induced note beneath the stage may be compared with Tideglass water pressures without claiming a magical cause. Greg's actual van, car wash, studio, and local newspaper remain in **Beach City**, reachable by voluntary travel rather than being placed on Starwake's street map.

## Homeworld loops tied to the six romances

**Ba Sing Se / Jasmine Dragon.** White Lotus discussions can be private and serious without turning Jasper into an involuntary agent. Mina has working hours, fair wages and inventory authority. Lian can demand that Iroh fix water damage to their shared wall. The garden's unidentified vine is labeled **NOT TEA** until identified. Lu Ten's remembrance includes grief, laughter and responsibility for past harm; it is not a romantic initiation ceremony.

**Beach City.** Printed mistakes require public correction even if old copies remain. The reopened community center can reject a donor whose money would purchase control of artists; the consequence is slower repairs and a published budget. Greg can write a second verse without erasing Rose. Jasper may consider work elsewhere; neither polyamory nor affection gives anyone veto power over that decision.

**Southern Water Tribe clinic.** Katara's trainees keep treating patients when she leaves. Supply requests have identifiable senders and sensible delivery windows; the Threadway keeper must agree to carrying anything. Katara, Aang, Toph and Jasper make non-exclusive, revisable commitments without collapsing into one personality or one calendar.

**Postwar wizarding world.** Confidential testimony remains confidential unless the witness freely approves a release. A researcher's uncertainty is a statement about evidence, not failure or cowardice. Severus may set his own grief boundaries without being transformed into a generous public speaker.

**Commonwealth / Brasswatch.** Danse's old rank has no authority over civilian partners, employees or the vale's council. Safety rules must concern behavior and evidence, not identity or previous affiliation.

## Four seasonal rhythms, seven districts

**Spring:** road mud, drain inspections, seed-sharing and damp archival paper create several small overlapping needs. **Summer:** water rationing, late public concerts, repaired shade cloth and deliveries before heat matter. **Autumn:** harvest storage, screened runoff, lantern maintenance and budget meetings set up winter safety. **Winter:** the Frostturn walk needs a clear turning route, warm shelters, inventory of blankets and voluntary ways to honor the dead. Festivals do not suspend wages or accessibility restrictions.

Crossovers should arise naturally from a shared object or task: an archive receipt, borrowed tool, corrected notice, tide reading, repaired stage plank, safely scheduled visit, or a song heard through the arch. Characters carry the consequences into their own novels. A cameo is not a substitute for conflict, consent or a finished scene.

## Relationships remain choices, not a village mechanic

Jasper is the same adult first-person protagonist and explicitly tells each partner that they are ethically non-monogamous and prefer polyamory. Partners may ask for time, privacy, practical reassurance or a changed boundary. They may decline an invitation or choose to spend an evening alone. No public board may list their private schedule. Sensual scenes can include shy invitations, awkward laughter, deliberate kisses, warmth and mutually desired closed doors; they remain non-explicit and never imply automatic consent later.

## Unresolved civic threads (seeds only; do not retroactively canonize)

Who pays for drain inspections after the first winter? What happens when a correction to a public map reveals that an older detour was unusable? How can the pier host free music without expecting unpaid labor from the same few neighbors? Can the archive write an honest history while leaving a disputed name blank? These questions invite later scenes, but **no answer has been silently written into the previous 300 chapters**.
