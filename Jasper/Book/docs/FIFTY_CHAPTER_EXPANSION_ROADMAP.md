# Fifty-Chapter Expansion Roadmap — Six Lanternweald Novels

**Status: editorial plan, not a claim that every planned chapter has been written.** Existing chapter files remain preserved. This roadmap separates authored material from proposed new scenes and gives each title a concrete narrative purpose.

**Quality floor for each full chapter:** aim 1,500–2,200 words when the dramatic work requires it; every chapter needs a change in relationship, plot or character understanding; several distinct scenes; character-specific dialogue; a physical environment that matters; and an ending that earns the next chapter. Do not pad a chapter merely to hit a number. Chapters shorter than 1,000 words should be examined and expanded organically rather than duplicated. For Maui, the ten existing shorter chapters especially need revision.

**Relationship rule:** Jasper tells every prospective partner that they practice ethical non-monogamy and prefer polyamory. No blanket consent from the cast, no instant agreement and no romantic relationship created by a doorway. Existing promises must be reconciled on-page. Each mature relationship gets one substantial, tender, sensual but non-graphic first private interlude and later varied fade-to-black transitions.

## Cross-book sequencing

The first ten Maui chapters precede the five other romances in Jasper's personal chronology. The additional chapters depict a later shared season, with letter references, scheduled visits and crossovers that do not require Jasper to be in two places at once. The order of publication does not imply that all six added chapters numbered 41 take place simultaneously. Each series ends by protecting homeworld commitments and Jasper's right to keep choosing, not by forcing a collective romance.
