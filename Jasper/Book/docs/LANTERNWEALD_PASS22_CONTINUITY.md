# Lanternweald Living-World Continuity — Pass 22

**Scope:** Directly grounded in the canonical chapter-6, chapter-19 and chapter-36 prose revisions made in all six novels on November 6, 2026. No new world branch, future prophecy, portal rules, or unwritten scenes are implied.

## Fundamental shared-world constraints

Lanternweald is a lived-in connective world, not an excuse to erase the individual history and practical rules of the six source settings. The crossings to Ba Sing Se, Cranefish Town and the Southern Water Tribe, the Commonwealth, Hogwarts and Beach City are meaningful because travelers must still respect their inhabitants and skills. Magic, bending, battle training, physical strength and famous reputations cannot replace licensed repair, shared governance, consent, or food and labor costs.

The old **lower Closed Bells maintenance passage remains sealed**. The erased keeper name is **still unknown**. These limits were not altered by Pass 22.

Jasper is a first-person adult narrator in negotiated ethically non-monogamous/polyamorous relationships. Each partner is an individual; separate apartments, appointments, calendars, histories, grief, instruments, records and public reputations remain theirs to govern. A relationship can be intimate and sensual without public disclosure or explicit narration.

## New scene connections

### Ba Sing Se / Jasmine Steps / Moonroot

- **Jasmine After Rain 6:** The broken Pai Sho tile's alleged White Lotus origin is unverified. A boy and his grandfather retain the right to explain its history, and the owner may seek a real carpenter. Iroh cannot turn his reputation into archival proof.
- **19:** A key to one of Jasper's separate spaces remains Jasper's to use. A private room above a working tea shop does not automatically require Jasper to be on call.
- **36:** Iroh knows about Severus and Moonroot and is jealous of work they share, but does not demand exclusivity. Jasper explicitly describes ethical non-monogamy and preference for polyamory; one ordinary Friday can be requested without trying to win the relationship.

**Iroh:** Carefully chosen words, strategic habits, grief over Lu Ten, confidence enough to be wrong, loving attention to details and tea; not Greg with more elegant phrasing.

### Cranefish Town / the Air Nomad school

- **Love & Duty 6:** Toph's self-defense training accompanies a safety review of a real loading-platform hoist. She can detect dangerous forces without rewriting the foreman's responsibilities.
- **19:** Aang is not automatically every school's plumbing officer because he founded a cultural institution. Paid staff handle maintenance and calendars. Jasper has real exhaustion rather than instantly recovering after delegation.
- **36:** No student or mutual-aid applicant has to prove Air Nomad bloodline to receive appropriate educational or housing aid. Katara protects the confidentiality of the school file. The fund remains about people who need help and agreed community practices.

**Aang:** Committed to culture without bloodline essentialism, sometimes hopeful to the point of overcommitment. **Katara:** Practical medical and family care with boundaries. **Toph:** Blunt material competence and irreverence, not an omniscient guardian.

**Mei** is a *fourteen-year-old original student*, not a love interest and not franchise canon's Mai. Her parents and uncle are specified in the original *Love & Duty* 36 manuscript; she decides within age-appropriate supervised care.

### Commonwealth / Brasswatch / Starwake Door

- **What the Armor Leaves Behind 6:** Haylen can give a realistic transmitter pickup window; command cannot simply demand a vertibird appear.
- **19:** The Prydwen's radstorm includes crew checks and credible fear, not romantic danger artificially resolved by rank.
- **36:** A *new shared-world scene* places Greg with Danse on the latter's porch to teach tuning and the cause of a buzzing guitar string. Greg's informal musical expertise and Danse's painstaking military vocabulary make it unmistakable that they are **different people**.

**Greg** is not a strategist, philosopher or soldier. He knows the feel of a badly set instrument, tells untidy jokes and may think about his own old manager. **Danse** analyzes failures as shortcomings of a plan or tool before he recognizes uncertainty and emotional risk. Their meeting cannot be retroactively treated as an official *Steven Universe* or *Fallout 4* event.

### Hogwarts / Moonroot

- **After Always 6:** Snape's recorded potion failures and laboratory shielding are substantive science, not scenery for generic wise remarks.
- **19:** His private, prickly intimacy is communicated through research annotations, a better quill and a small touch in the face of an unresolved threatening letter.
- **36:** Minerva, not Jasper, demands a viable student complaints process. Severus helps define a response period and independent reviewer. Professor Sinistra is a lesson observer in **fanfiction**; her verified canon responsibility is teaching Astronomy at Hogwarts.

**Severus** is sometimes cruel and has to account for harm. A school appointment does not cancel old harm. He remains precise, emotionally guarded and caustic even while learning safer professional practices. **Sinistra** is an Astronomy professor with established knowledge of star charts and planetary motion, not a canon-described Potions regulator or love interest.

### Beach City / Starwake Pier

- **Stars Between the Strings 6:** Greg's immediate domestic behavior after a private night includes coffee, borrowed clothes, corny jokes and explicitly chosen physical closeness.
- **19:** A new apartment does not belong to Greg just because Jasper loves him. Jasper repeats that they are ethically non-monogamous and prefer polyamory; jealous feelings are allowed, possession is not.
- **36:** A stranger wants a song suggesting Greg has finally “settled down.” Greg rejects the premise without repudiating romance. He has been a musician, a van owner and Steven's father before and during this relationship.

The very same Greg can repair a musical instrument for Danse, drop a tape measure during a kiss and be embarrassed by a stage request. None of these behaviors needs a rehearsed moral speech.

### Lanternwell / Tideglass

- **The Shore I Choose 6:** Mereana and her visiting group, not Maui, control how their fragile instruments are packed, carried and displayed. **Mereana explicitly identifies herself as a Māori performer and teacher from Aotearoa** in chapter 6. Her specific iwi and private rituals remain **unstated**, so do not invent them.
- **19:** **Lio is sixteen**, an original boatbuilding apprentice competent to direct Maui's hands while preparing Sela's skiff. Lio must never become a sensual, adult, or romantic character.
- **36:** Mara, Maui and Jasper respond to public gossip about Jasper's other partners without forcing a public confession, an unwanted speech or a retaliatory spectacle. The bakery's public board has a legitimate purpose separate from private correspondence.

**Maui** is physically extraordinary, proud, big-voiced, capable of humility through action, and constantly challenged by a Mini Maui whose joke often undercuts him. He is not a mild-mannered lecturer.

## On-page canon versus research

Research only creates evidence about established franchise characters; it does not fabricate missing personal details for originals. Pass 22 added official-source grounded **Professor Sinistra** and manuscript-grounded **Mei** profiles. It corrected Mereana's prior omission of her explicitly stated Māori identity and Lio's omitted age. As a result the active character index records **170 profiles**. Earlier Pass 21 audit and continuity files now carry visible corrections rather than continuing to assert that Mereana's identity is unknown.

## Potential future crossovers — not yet written

A later visit might let Professor Sinistra observe how another world's star maps differ without using a generic shared sky, and might let Greg help another musician while Danse maintains independent mechanical safety rules. A Pai Sho tile might become a genuine historical record only with the original owner's testimony or verifiable archival evidence. A later Air Nomad class might revisit eligibility rules with participants rather than with a famous face alone. **None of these events occurred in Pass 22**.

Keep the existing six 50-chapter arcs intact. Continue to let dialogue, physical intimacy, mutually informed choices and ordinary-world consequences carry different emotional trajectories instead of producing the same soothing resolution in every relationship.
