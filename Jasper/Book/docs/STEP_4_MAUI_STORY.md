# Step 4 — Maui and Jasper integrated

The Shore I Choose is a newly authored, complete ten-chapter linear novel. It begins at Tideglass Cove before the first five story threads open. Jasper's identity returns in fragments; Maui supports but does not decide their choices. The plot includes maritime adventures, emotional growth, non-explicit romance, and a culturally contextualized contemporary poi/love-song performance with source notes. There are now six complete stories and 210 chapters in the offline site. Original five stories and their 25 crossover bridges remain intact.
