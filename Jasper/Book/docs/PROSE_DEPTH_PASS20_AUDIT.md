## Prose changes, not just word padding

### Iroh — *Jasmine After Rain*

- **08:** Ba Sing Se's wall is no longer merely a metaphor for belonging. Iroh encounters a laborer who remembers his military reputation. He refuses to certify cracked masonry based on old battlefield knowledge; laborers must be allowed to present their own measurements.
- **18:** The preexisting closing-scene repetition was repaired, including removal of a duplicate final line. Mina's unpaid closing time is corrected and the shop's posted hours must reflect actual work. Romance no longer excuses invisible staff labor.
- **45:** A suspicious extra carriage fee for scarce mountain mint is verified with growers; Iroh and Mina refuse to turn an ordinary fair trade transaction into a heroic speech.

**Voice:** Iroh anticipates costs and routes and can be dangerously overprotective. His warm economy, military habit, tea-shop responsibility and grief do not turn him into Greg's generic wise companion.

### Aang, Katara and Toph — *Love & Duty*

- **08:** The celebratory footbridge ribbon-cutting stops when Toph questions a rail's actual safety. Katara arranges the clinic detour, and Aang declines to treat the Avatar's personal popularity as engineering approval. The existing first-kiss boundaries remain.
- **18:** A generous Air Nomad restoration gift carries unacceptable dynastic conditions. Aang and Katara disagree productively over funding; a smaller voluntary classroom with wages, meals and named teachers becomes the practicable next step.
- **42:** A polyamorous schedule is not a merger of other partners into interchangeable hours. Jasper explicitly states ethical non-monogamy and preference for polyamory again, while Toph protects rest, Katara names real medical work, and Aang admits his preparations need time.

**Voice:** Aang is playful but not passive. Katara presses toward practical care and accountability. Toph challenges structural assumptions and private schedules sharply, without being a plot-solving caricature.

### Danse and Haylen — *What the Armor Leaves Behind*

- **08:** The summarized mission became an on-page relay-station encounter where Danse and Jasper test their agreed field positions and retreat rules in actual danger; a bystander escapes without a false guarantee of escort.
- **18:** Haylen confronts the people talking over her about her broken wrist. She independently chooses allowable work and roster hours; Danse adjusts rather than treating authority as concern.
- **42:** Renn cautions Danse that an old courier seal reveals the network used. His personal reply to Haylen stays out of the public workshop records; real repair work continues.

**Voice:** His precise procedural habits remain useful, but romance cannot transfer command over Jasper, Haylen or Renn.

### Severus Snape — *After Always*

- **08:** Repairing a vandalized table after taking a photograph still damages original evidence. Jasper and Severus document the alteration accurately and do not present a magical cleanup as flawless.
- **18:** An awkward, rain-soaked date includes Severus's injured hand and sarcastic resistance to helpfulness. The existing explicit early ethical-non-monogamy conversation remains a choice, not a contract signed after dinner.
- **42:** Iroh returns for a basket while Neri has the disputed Closed Bells map. The former general and the Potions scholar disagree about the meaning of a missing line; Neri controls the archive's public presentation and neither fabricates the keeper's identity.

**Voice:** Severus notices contamination, provenance, language and pride. Iroh notices historic strategic temptation; Greg would notice a faulty sound cable. Their expertise and humor are not interchangeable.

### Greg Universe — *Stars Between the Strings*

- **08:** Jasper realizes a homemade shaker belongs to the community center and asks Pilar about a real lending system. Greg's support is silly, musical and grounded in access to rehearsal equipment.
- **18:** Greg sends Steven a safe check-in, shows his unsolved anxiety in the form of a real van and pancake breakfast, and understands that loving Jasper nonexclusively does not determine where he must live.
- **42:** Greg, Danse and Maui handle separate stage risks: grounded wiring and amplifier limits, structural approval, and unlit lanterns. The fundraiser has a real technician and a smaller safe performance area.

**Voice:** Informal funny musician and father, capable of technical judgment but not smooth or omniscient. He is not Iroh's philosophical echo.

### Maui — *The Shore I Choose*

- **08:** Neri establishes Threadway log privacy before Jasper enters an old personal room. Maui's habitual desire to perform assistance meets an actual no, which he respects.
- **18:** Maui remains curious about Jasper's notebook but accepts that romance does not entitle him to each memory. His theatrical performance and Mini Maui humor remain while he refers to steering with Moana rather than demanding a legend.
- **42:** A high-tide test nearly destabilizes a temporary surface channel. Maui holds one beam on Edda's instructions while Fen, Toph and Tamsin direct proper measurements. The old Lower Closed Bells passage stays sealed.

**Voice:** Proud, boisterous, physical, skilled at being useful, vulnerable to becoming a spectacle; not a generic accommodating partner.

## Creative and continuity limits

This pass made substantive changes in **18** chapters, not every one of the 300. All six novels still have exactly 50 written chapters. Equality of chapter count or crossing a word threshold does not prove parity of depth. Maintain Jasper's first-person voice from `json/william-writing-style.json`, the recurring ethics of mutually informed polyamory, sensual but non-explicit intimacy and private fade-to-black, and each person's freedom to decline invitations.

Avoid formulaic reassurance, recycling the same closing phrase across love interests, and making Jasper responsible for every character's improvement. Whenever possible, let labor costs, historical evidence, expertise, travel permissions, privacy, schedules or physical risks create a new scene consequence that changes what the characters do next.
