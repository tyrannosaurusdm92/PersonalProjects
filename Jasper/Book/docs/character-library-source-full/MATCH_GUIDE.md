# Jasper Character Match Guide

Research/build date: 2026-09-20

Full JSON profiles separate canon grounding from fanfic/intimacy inference.

## Closest overall to older/wiser + goofy sweetheart + sappy
- **Hodari Pavel** — Possibly the strongest new match: official Palia material openly calls him a romance-shaped “Sad Dad”; widowed, middle-aged-coded, practical, protective, awkwardly flustered and slow to trust.
- **Barry Burton** — An almost perfect match: older veteran, weapons expert, devoted father, huge protective energy and canonically terrible puns under pressure.
- **Alfred Pennyworth** — Probably the strongest DC match: older veteran, dry-witted caretaker, conscience, field medic, manager and expert at brutal truths delivered with tea.
- **Lucifer Morningstar** — Ancient power plus theatrical silliness, awkward dad energy, idealism damaged by despair, and intense affection that often comes out sideways.
- **Pacha** — Big, patient, practical and genuinely kind; reaction-based comedy and family warmth make him an excellent domestic sweetheart template.
- **Bruce Banner / Hulk** — Soft-spoken scientist with enormous power, dry awkwardness and a long relationship with fear of losing control; gentleness is central, not decorative.
- **Beckett** — Former raider trying to be better, bartender, romanceable ally and someone whose committed dialogue becomes endearingly earnest/corny.
- **King Fergus** — Peak loud older-warrior dad: heroic protector, imposing, impulsive, story-loving, funny and deeply devoted to family.
- **Wataru** — Retired boat captain, father, lighthouse resident and gardener: pure lived-in quiet-routine romance material.

## Greg branch — warm, funny, visibly affectionate
- **Scott Lang / Ant-Man** — One of the cleanest MCU matches: adult dad, improviser, affectionate, anxious, funny and driven by love for family rather than grandeur.
- **Badruu** — Greg energy turned into agriculture: farmer, relentless vegetable puns, former bard, sentimental romantic and supportive family man whose humor shields heavier feelings.
- **Kronk** — Maximum goofy-sweetheart: huge, gentle, earnest, cooking-focused, multitalented and canonically described by Disney as a lovable lug.
- **Genie** — Pure ancient-but-goofy sweetheart: joke-cracking, shape-changing, loyal and emotionally centered on freedom and chosen attachment.
- **Sir Pentious** — Bombastic villain posturing, mechanical brilliance, deep loneliness, literal-minded comedy, huge feelings and a very earnest capacity for attachment.
- **Reth** — Younger than the core preference but excellent goofy-heart material: talented chef, devoted brother, fast mouth, impulsive humor and hidden burdens.
- **Happy Hogan** — Older everyday-guy sweetheart: loyal, protective, easily flustered, sometimes grouchy and much more comfortable handling logistics than grand declarations.
- **Arthur Curry / Aquaman** — Giant goofy-warrior option: imposing, irreverent, family-driven, uncomfortable with royalty and capable of leadership without losing scruffy outsider humor.
- **Kristoff** — Younger than the core older-man niche but a strong rugged-sweetheart match: practical, awkward at romance, deeply loyal and sincere enough that bungled proposals feel endearing.

## Hard exclusions
- Real people, actors, voice actors, creators, celebrities, streamers and performer personas.
- Any romantic/sexual participant under 18.
- Original-series minor Aang, Toph or Katara in romantic/sexual content.
- Billy Batson/Shazam in adult sexual content: an adult-looking magical body does not make the child person/mind an adult participant.
- Any genuinely ambiguous-age character until the selected story metadata explicitly establishes age 18+.

## Canon-state handling
Married, partnered, deceased and non-romanceable characters are labeled in their profiles. Choose AU, canon divergence or consensual poly context rather than silently erasing those facts.
