# Lanternweald Pass 16 — The Work That Carries Across Books

**Canon status:** These notes track **scenes now written** in existing chapters, and distinguish them from possible future developments. They extend the living-world bible without replacing it. Crossovers are voluntary visits from continuing homeworlds; their scene dates must not be assumed identical merely because they share a motif.

## 1. Lanternwell Square: a public board with limits

- **Written:** The arrival spring's crack is monitored by Tamsin and recorded by Neri (Maui 3). Maui cannot make the spring safer by proclaiming a feat of strength.
- **Written:** Mara's bakery chalkboard becomes a rumor wall. Jasper removes the names; Pip requires consent and payment for a separate civic board; Neri may raise the proposal for proper approval (Maui 36). **The separate board is proposed, not yet canonically installed.**
- **World consequence:** Residents still need posted bread prices, tide warnings and accessible notices. A boundary against gossip has material costs, not an infinite free painter.
- **Further story opportunity, not enacted:** Pip's funded sign-return day could fix incorrect festival times with a public correction log; it must avoid photographing anyone who declines.

## 2. Tideglass: safe passage is more than a door

- **Written:** A departing traveler receives a map without their identity and is free to decline work at the bakery (Maui 12).
- **Written:** Tomas uses a bridge whose printed diagram is out of date; Neri records the error for Edda, while Maui doesn't take his luggage or announce his destination (Maui 27).
- **World consequence:** Edda controls structural/turning review, Fen maintains time-specific tides, and visitors control the nature of their departures. The Threadway doesn't punish refusal. The old sealed underground flood passage remains sealed.
- **Further story opportunity, not enacted:** a resident-authored map comparing handcarts, wheelchairs and ferry luggage routes. No universal "safe at all tides" stamp.

## 3. Moonroot: public evidence requires physical access

- **Written:** The ledger table is temporarily unstable and a chair blocks the turning area. Orin has a funded repair order; Neri approves temporary assistance, and Severus refuses to call the maintenance trivial (Maui 48).
- **Written:** Severus uses a stop signal during an Occlumency lesson and records only that the student ended the session; nothing from Jasper's unshared memories enters the record (After Always 23).
- **World consequence:** Evidence is not access merely because a volume exists. Record permissions, staff labor, readable surfaces and correction routes must all work. The Closed Bells keeper is still unidentified.
- **Further story opportunity, not enacted:** a demonstration of corrected annotations where source and inference are visually distinct and a reader may decline to disclose their identity.

## 4. Jasmine Steps and real Ba Sing Se: warmth with an invoice

- **Written:** Mina rejects an impossible rota, Iroh learns that supplies and wages require agreement before a plan is finalized (Jasmine 12).
- **Written:** Jun's relief kitchen has three-day late rice and autonomy over its service; Iroh can carry cups, purchase a sack and face what his generalship did without replacing other people's choices (Jasmine 37).
- **World consequence:** The Jasmine Dragon stays in Ba Sing Se, while Jasmine Steps in Lanternweald is a district of distinct shops. Iroh is not Greg in robes: his instincts are strategic, his jokes come from food and history, and his failings can cost staff time.

## 5. Starwake and Beach City: a song has an owner and a load-out time

- **Written:** Greg's van repair needs an invoice, safe mechanics, payment and a realistic offer of help from Steven. Greg's comfort language is rambling music and practical jokes (Stars 16).
- **Written:** Nola's paid sound crew refuses uncontracted teardown. Greg keeps two sets rather than treating a fundraiser as endless availability, while Maui, Danse and Tamsin have distinct capacities and limits (Stars 42).
- **World consequence:** Starwake has finite stage load and scheduled shifts. Greg can leave an unfinished song unfinished and return to Beach City. Maui can be loud without commandeering another musician's stage.

## 6. Brasswatch and the Commonwealth: rank versus repeatable procedure

- **Written:** Danse signs a generator repair as Danse, not as Paladin, and teaches the local operator how to repeat the load test and dispute an inaccessible step (Armor 37).
- **World consequence:** The operator owns the operating handover and the repairer owns the recorded repair. Danse can have a name and work without claiming Brotherhood authority in an independent community.

## 7. Riverstone and the South: no cargo from nowhere

- **Written:** Aang's race test threatens Beren's work route; Toph and Sokka's hardware checks matter; Katara and Jasper account for who uses the crossing (Love & Duty 24).
- **Written:** The Southern clinic edits its requested supplies, and Katara refuses unneeded cargo. Aang, Toph and Jasper support her independently chosen trip rather than making her an adjunct to the relationship (Love & Duty 43).
- **World consequence:** The South Pole remains a real place with local healers, space and supply needs. Tideglass crops do not magically replace qualified medical care. Aang's creativity, Katara's clinical attention and Toph's physical insight are distinct voices.

## 8. Relationship and personality continuity

Jasper says plainly in existing romantic arcs that they are ethically non-monogamous and prefer polyamory. Nothing in this pass reverses that disclosure; the recurring conflict should be changed behavior, consent, separate schedules and time with actual partners, **not** a copied explanatory speech in every chapter. Adult flirtation and chosen privacy may lead to non-explicit intimacy or a fade to black. Maui's swagger, desire for applause, hook, quick jokes and ties to Moana are never discarded to make him a flawless partner.

These are **seeds**, not events that have already occurred. Future prose may reject or alter them if the canonical manuscript makes another choice.
