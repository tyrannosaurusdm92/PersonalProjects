# Jasper Reader and Story Bible

Jasper is an adult, named, first-person reader-insert protagonist. Narration uses I/me/my/mine/myself. Jasper practices ethical non-monogamy and prefers polyamory; each romantic relationship requires a separate, plain-language discussion of consent and boundaries, and no partner is assumed to agree automatically. Jasper is observant, tender, witty, stubborn, and has a right to their own boundaries. Never replace them with a blank choice menu.

## Connected stories
- After Always: Severus Snape retains his difficult postwar history, precision, privacy, accountability, and dry humor.
- Jasmine After Rain: original-series Uncle Iroh retains his tea, Pai Sho, grief for Lu Ten, strategic intelligence, compassion, and joy.
- Love & Duty: the adult Aang, Katara, Toph and Jasper relationship preserves its consensual dynamics, public obligations, political conflict, and lived repair.
- What the Armor Leaves Behind: Danse's rank, Brotherhood past and moral struggle remain consequential; a magical door cannot erase them.
- Stars Between the Strings: Greg's music, grief, Beach City ties, family, and ability to choose love remain his own.

The Lanternweald connects their settings via voluntary Threadways, not parallel branching romances. Relationship boundaries and consent remain particular to each story; no crossover presumes access to another character.

## New continuity and chapter-depth standard
Six complete original arcs are followed by a planned 50-chapter total for each story. The first new continuation chapters are written; the remaining chapter-by-chapter plot is outlined in `50_CHAPTER_EXPANSION_ROADMAP.md` and must not be presented as published prose. The Seventh Ring crisis links the six universes through material and social consequences; no single lover, keeper, magical key, or chosen-one trope substitutes for communal repair.

For intimacy, write attraction through distinct voices, lingering gestures, flirtation, awkward humor, explicit permission when needed, and genuine character conflict. Keep private scenes non-graphic, with natural fades to black rather than abrupt placeholders. Earlier conversations about polyamory must continue to have consequences as partners articulate their independent preferences, needs, and uncertainty.

## Rating and craft
Teen-rated, non-graphic intimacy, consent and natural privacy transitions. No adult content generator, branching story outcome, private scene stub, or choose-your-own-adventure mechanics. The reader offers fixed chapters with ordinary Next/Previous navigation.
