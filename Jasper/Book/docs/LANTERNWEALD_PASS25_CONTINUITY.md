# Lanternweald Continuity — Pass 25: The Work Between Worlds
**November 7, 2026.** This records newly written on-page additions to chapter 17 and chapter 42 of each novel and clarifies boundaries already established in previous chapters.

## The difference between a meeting place and a story machine
The Lanternweald is not a portal where every visitor becomes the same gentle mentor or where Jasper's love silently fixes an institution. A person can come through the Threadway for tea, song, an inspection, or a private visit; they bring their established history, professional limits, loyalties and social awkwardness with them. No one can claim another's shift, borrowed equipment, affection or private correspondence just because they helped during an emergency.

**Chapter 17** in each novel remains rooted in homeworld development, not an invented all-six gathering. In Ba Sing Se, Mina keeps the Jasmine Dragon functional and expects wages; in Cranefish Town, Aang builds a new remembrance gathering without pretending a destroyed tradition was perfectly preserved; Severus allows help with his hand without surrendering control of it; Haylen challenges an untested sight in the Commonwealth, and Danse takes evidence seriously; Greg asks questions about a job contract rather than making Jasper's decision for them; at Tideglass, Fen removes an unsafe lantern fixture and Maui learns that holding a ladder may be the right kind of heroism.

**Chapter 42** brings different professional voices together at shared-world locations without merging their personalities. At Six Tables, Iroh thinks in movement routes and fair work, while Greg thinks in soundchecks and musical timing. At Riverstone, Katara protects patients, Toph measures structural limits, Aang questions the council's authority, and Jasper can decline treating a polyamorous household like a mandatory rota. At Moonroot, Severus considers the price of performing sociability; he need not imitate Iroh to understand him. At Brasswatch, a letter from Haylen remains Danse's private property while water samples still require testing. At Starwake, Nola, Rowan, Tamsin and paid staff keep a fundraiser running when Greg cannot do every job alone. At Tideglass, Sela's receipts, Fen's dates, Toph's ground-sense and Tamsin's inspections have standing independent of Maui's strength.

## The public spaces have calendars, costs and limits
**Lanternwell Square:** Before a shared meal, the posted table plan must include an unobstructed passage, washing arrangements, allergy/ingredient information when known, adequate rest seating and an actual cleanup roster. Free meals are gifts of hospitality, not unrecorded free labor from Mina, Mara or volunteers. A guest may take food without surrendering private information or being made into a moral example.

**Jasmine Steps / Ba Sing Se:** Iroh's tea expertise is skilled work. The same is true of Mina's shift schedule, Ren's time off, a supplier's invoice and a potter's kiln safety. Iroh may set strategy and apologize; neither action substitutes for paying wages or fixing a dangerous cup.

**Riverstone:** A bridge inspection happens when the light and the ground permit it, not when lovers need another Tuesday. Clinic appointments belong to patients who did not consent to have their care rearranged for a romantic subplot. The Avatar's fame cannot serve as residents' legal signature.

**Moonroot:** Invitation letters and source materials are different from public testimony. Severus can accept or refuse a social event. A report should distinguish observation from inference, and no one is entitled to private notes about Lily or an unidentified keeper. The lower Closed Bells remains sealed; the erased line remains unnamed.

**Brasswatch:** A purifier's working light is not a clean-water test. Aster and other residents need dated water results, repeated readings and a person answerable for failures. Danse's safety instincts serve civilians most effectively when he permits qualified civilians to challenge him.

**Starwake:** The amplifier, accessible stage route and paid technicians have their own schedules. A volunteer sheet is not a license to make Greg or anybody else carry every chair. Rowan's paid sound check, Nola's booking rules and Tamsin's inspection decisions continue after the last song.

**Tideglass:** The sea takes little notice of heroic speeches. Fen can close a mooring or lantern hook; Tamsin can refuse to certify a beam; Sela can demand the market receive results in writing. The upper temporary surface channel has its own risk range and tests. **Never conflate it with the sealed lower historic passage**, and never disclose Jasper's private lantern names as a condition of safety.

## Cross-novel resonances without identical dialogue
- Iroh and Greg are both generous but have different expertise, cadence, training and blind spots. A shared cup of citrus-and-smoke tea can be a joke about sound to Greg and an analysis of leaf quality and drying temperature to Iroh.
- Danse and Severus both value privacy, but one makes practical inspection logs and the other guards historical letters and potions with an educator's exactness.
- Maui and Aang may both want to help immediately, but Maui's concern is often his need to be indispensable, whereas Aang must reckon with cultural loss and others using the Avatar's authority.
- Toph and Fen can disagree without one being framed as magically wrong: the ground's incline and the tide's measurement are separate necessary facts.
- Jasper's love is not ownership. Explicit adult conversations about ethical non-monogamy and preferred polyamory remain part of the existing chapters. Respect negotiated schedules, embarrassment, attraction, reassurance and the right to say no.

Future scenes may return to these routines, but no as-yet unwritten event is retroactively asserted to have happened. Existing chronology, homeworld boundaries and independently working residents remain governing canon.
