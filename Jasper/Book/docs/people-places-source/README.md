# People & Places Generator

A project-neutral generator and lightweight simulator for **people and locations** across realistic, fantasy, and science-fiction settings.

Open `generator.html`. The project intentionally contains no avatar creator, portrait generator, sprite system, mesh/body renderer, pose library, visual equipment anchoring, or image-asset pipeline. Physical traits are descriptive person data only.

## Folder contract

The package root contains exactly:

- `generator.html`
- `js/`
- `css/`
- `json/`
- `docs/`

No nested directories are required.

## People systems

Names, pronouns, gender identities, personality, values, moods, hobbies, skills, work/education, relationships, routines, memory, knowledge, conversations, life events, and text-only physical descriptions.

## Location systems

Realistic residences/workplaces/schools plus fantasy settlements, taverns, guilds, academies and magical sites, and science-fiction stations, starships, colonies, asteroid environments, planetary sites and related infrastructure.
