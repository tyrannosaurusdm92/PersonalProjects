# Lanternweald Continuity — Pass 20

**Updated:** 2026-11-06  
**Authority:** This guide summarizes the 18 existing chapter files edited in Pass 20. It does not redefine prior canon, create new chapters, move a world gate, or claim story events that have not happened on the page.

## Foundational continuity

Lanternweald's seven neighborhoods remain an inhabited, economically constrained shared world connecting distinct original/crossover settings. A world gate does not erase the legal, cultural, technical or environmental rules of the world on either side. Jasper is an adult first-person narrator with ethical non-monogamy and a preference for polyamory. These relationships are consensual, negotiated, and not interchangeable; each partner can ask for privacy, alone time, safety and dedicated dates without gaining authority over other people.

The lower Closed Bells maintenance passage is **still sealed**. The erased historic keeper's identity is **still unknown**. A map line, a recollection or an oral statement may be an investigatory lead without being proof. Voluntary traveler Threadways and structurally unsound maintenance works are different infrastructure.

## On-page district links and consequences

### Jasmine Steps and the Jasmine Dragon

In *Jasmine After Rain* 8, Iroh's history with Ba Sing Se makes him hesitate when laborers seek an inspection of cracked city-wall masonry. His experience as a general is not a surveyor's technical measurement. In 18, Mina corrects the staff wages and posted hours after an early closing; the ending no longer repeats the same atmospheric sentence. In 45, the mountain-mint carriage invoice is verified against what growers actually agreed to pay. A tea shop cannot buy forgiveness with loose bookkeeping.

**Character guardrails:** Iroh may be tactical and patient and still initially assign people tasks without asking; Mina is not rude for demanding accuracy. Greg would never describe a damaged wall as a maneuver to be won simply because he wants to be useful. No trade fee or safety inspection is solved by a moral maxim.

### Riverstone, Cranefish Town and the Air Nomad school

In *Love & Duty* 8, Aang stops an opening speech when Toph questions the eastern footbridge's rail safety and Katara demands a clinic-safe detour. In 18, the proposed Air Nomad temple gift has dynastic terms that violate cultural autonomy; the immediate alternative is a smaller, budgeted classroom whose teacher must be asked to participate. In 42, Jasper's and the other partners' rest, time and relationships remain individually negotiated, and Jasper openly reaffirms their ethical non-monogamy and preference for polyamory.

**Character guardrails:** Aang can be joyful and firm without forcing the future into a recreation of the past. Katara's work at a clinic gives her a different authority from Toph's knowledge of material structure. Toph does not have magical permission to volunteer other people's time or monitor everyone's private choices. This group cannot solve every problem by voting at breakfast.

### Brasswatch, Commonwealth and the old Brotherhood

In *What the Armor Leaves Behind* 8, the field-position agreement between Jasper and Danse is tested during a relay-station retreat rather than merely explained. In 18, Haylen challenges a rota created around her injured wrist and selects medically appropriate assignments herself. In 42, Renn identifies the risk that an old Brotherhood courier seal can expose a private channel. Danse keeps Haylen's letter out of public workshop records and returns to an actual repair.

**Character guardrails:** Danse does not automatically earn permission to command a shop because he has rank or experience. Haylen has training and boundaries. Renn protects workshop records but is not obligated to be anybody's intelligence courier. An old military mark can threaten people outside the military.

### Spinner's End, Hogsmeade and Moonroot Archive

In *After Always* 8, Jasper admits to modifying an original piece of vandalism evidence, and Severus documents the deficiency instead of rewarding the impulsive protection. In 18, his injured hand gives a rain-soaked date its awkward, non-explicit physical reality; the polyamory conversation remains a choice, not a contract. In 42, Neri's disputed Closed Bells map draws different reactions from Iroh and Severus. Iroh remembers the danger of marching troops along unverified lines. Severus insists on provenance and refuses to guess the mapmaker's notation. Neri limits what may be exposed to the public.

**Character guardrails:** Severus may be interested in evidence and protective of Jasper while remaining sarcastic, difficult, and legally accountable for his earlier conduct. Iroh does not become a second Potions professor, and Severus is not a taciturn tea philosopher. Neri's public-interest duty does not grant them permission to publish someone's private correspondence.

### Starwake Pier and Beach City

In *Stars Between the Strings* 8, Greg recognizes a community-center loaned instrument and supports Jasper asking Pilar for a proper checkout. In 18, he checks in with Steven and explains what the van means to him without making Jasper's polyamory a choice of housing. In 42, Greg, Danse and Maui share a fundraiser but perform different functions. Greg understands sound equipment, Danse requires actual load measurements, and Maui must respect an untested structural limit. Greg can play a smaller, safer set without feeling that he is personally diminished.

**Character guardrails:** Greg's hair, van, former car wash, songs, past with Rose and fatherhood should remain his own history, not overwritten by a shared-world romance. He talks like an informal musician. Past Greg manager Marty, his son Sour Cream, Vidalia and Yellowtail now have researched references; do not manufacture scenes or new family relationships because a reference file exists.

### Lanternwell Square and Tideglass

In *The Shore I Choose* 8, Neri explains that a private Threadway crossing can be logged without copying the contents of Jasper's old room. Maui tries to volunteer himself, hears no and waits. In 18, he does not open a private notebook even after Jasper chooses closeness. He connects the difficulty of simply steering a boat when Moana needs him with the temptation to be a legend. In 42, a high-tide structural test requires Maui to stabilize a **single beam** while Fen, Toph, Edda and Tamsin make separate engineering judgments. The lower Closed Bells route remains closed, and the new temporary upper channel's operating range is narrower after the test.

**Character guardrails:** Maui can make a flamboyant joke while doing unglamorous work. Mini Maui is a mischievous extension of him, not an all-knowing conscience. Local workers retain their names, wages, expertise and right to stop a test.
