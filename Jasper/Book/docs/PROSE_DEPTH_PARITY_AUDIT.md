## Editorial quality checks for future passes

1. **Read for action and changed decisions**, not only word count. Every expanded scene should offer a concrete interaction or complication; avoid padding scenes with abstract statements that repeat the theme.
2. **Vary each partner's dialogue.** Maui can joke broadly but his jealousy matters; Severus is precise and dry; Iroh gentle but not endlessly wise; Aang hopeful, Katara decisive and Toph blunt; Danse deliberate, recovering from command; Greg funny and privately uncertain. Jasper has a recognizable first-person cadence across all six.
3. **Keep the world a place with ordinary obligations.** Tea suppliers, permit fees, volunteer shifts, rainy bridges, grain shortages, accessible routes, privacy, tools and notes create depth. Residents must retain schedules, consent and limits beyond Jasper's plot.
4. **Protect early-to-late continuity.** Early Maui chapter 5 concerns the misaligned lower Tideglass **traveler Threadway**. Later episodes concern the distinct, historic **underground drainage flood barrier**, which remains sealed. The separate surface overflow passes tests after the storms.
5. **Maintain ethical non-monogamy explicitly and responsibly.** Jasper has told their partners they are ethically non-monogamous and prefer polyamory. Further revisions cannot replace consent with exclusivity, partner veto, unrequested disclosure or a requirement that partners romance each other.
6. **Make intimacy earned and non-graphic.** Specific awkward flirting, private jokes, mutual requests and unforced fade-to-black should vary. Avoid generic, repeated kissing descriptions, repetitive wisdom speeches and mandatory perfect reconciliation.
7. **Keep the offline reader genuinely offline.** Change canonical JSON, update the matching manifest and catalog totals, then copy revised content into an overlay in script order. Never remove the original floating-book entry interface.
8. **Validate source and reader independently from visual usability.** Unique chapter IDs, 1–50 numbering and next links can be tested structurally; visual layout must be checked separately in an actual browser.
