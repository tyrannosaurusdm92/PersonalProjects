## Story constraints retained

Jasper narrates in the first person as an adult, has explicitly disclosed ethical non-monogamy and a preference for polyamory, and chooses relationships without exclusivity being presumed. Romance remains consensual and non-explicit, with occasional fade-to-black moments. All six original plots, chapter IDs, titles, order, story endings and independent partner relationships are retained. The historic underground flood passage remains sealed. Māori traditions and contemporary artists are not interchangeable with Disney's fictional hook, tattoos or transformation powers; no sacred repertoire or invented iwi-specific practice was inserted.

## Data integrity and limits

The nine changed source chapter entries were generated from their existing files and the website override was generated from those same revised chapter objects. Their chapter numbers, IDs, titles, contents and word counts were synchronized at construction. Every revised chapter remains over 1,200 words. The six manifest totals were recalculated from their respective 50 chapter entries, and the catalog's formerly stale per-series and library totals were reconciled. **This is nine revised chapters out of 300, not a claim of a complete literary proofread of all chapters.** The source change was not tested in a live browser or independently reviewed by a cultural consultant.
