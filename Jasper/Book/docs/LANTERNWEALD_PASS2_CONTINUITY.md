# Lanternweald: The Next Twelve Chapters — Continuity and World Lore

**Status:** Part of the same original shared world. This file clarifies events that appear in the second batch of finished prose without retconning the first world bible or falsely declaring the remaining outline entries complete.

## 1. Departing freely is a civic institution

Jasper discovers in Tideglass chapter 12 that a public arrival register can make vulnerable guests feel observed or trapped. Their conversation with **Lark**, a visitor using an alias, leads to a new protocol: a voluntarily supplied name, optional contact method, private safe-return arrangements, no public announcements without consent, and a voluntary note saying whether anyone should wait. There is **no expected-return obligation**. Neri retains records needed for maintenance and emergencies but has no right to compel disclosure of a visitor's next destination. Lark's decision to leave is a valid ending to a visit.

The safeguard has downstream plot value: Danse cannot use an old Brotherhood radio channel simply because someone he cares about might be listening, and **Ash** can request accommodation and repair work without publicly revealing their origin. Helpers cannot invent consent on behalf of the person helped. These are public-space decisions, not new supernatural powers.

## 2. The seventh scratch and the old water system

In Tideglass chapter 13, a battered page from the old maintenance ledger washes ashore. It shows a **half-moon-notched key** of the same *pattern* as Jasper's, an arch with seven marks, and a measured waterline. The pattern was shared by multiple maintenance keepers. The fragment therefore does **not** prove that Jasper once belonged to a secret royal family, owned the spring, or was fated to save anyone. Six scratches are visible from ordinary standing height; the seventh can only be seen low to the water.

The historic maintenance route is a **storm-drainage service passage under the spring**, not a seventh franchise universe or a seventh compulsory romance. The key is mechanical evidence with uncertain provenance. Neri remembers the Closed Bells period, when someone restricted exits in the name of safety. They are ashamed and reluctant to discuss it but cannot withhold current safety information; the community must involve independent engineers and other affected residents, not just accept Neri's personal memory.

**Scene-use rule:** Never let an old text, diagram, bell, or found key retroactively rewrite another character's memory, undo a homeworld consequence, or become a magical excuse for coercion.

## 3. The shared practical ecology

The pantry distributes food and tea according to actual stocks. It does not conjure endless leaves, flour or water. The **Six Tables** community feast now uses flexible seating instead of preassigning chairs according to imagined romantic ties. It has a safe preparation plan and allergy checks; strangers need not labor to earn a meal. People may decline company without being treated as disloyal. Children and adults from different worlds can share a square without ceasing to belong to their home communities.

The **Brasswatch water purifier** has a mechanical housing, intake pipe, replacement gasket and independent chemical tests. A mechanically sound replacement can still fail if the intake pipe introduces contaminants after an idle period. Danse and Severus contribute different expertise, and neither is the sole hero of the repair. Until repeated tests prove safety, the independent public hand pump remains in use with deliveries for people who cannot carry the water themselves.

A mixed repair economy requires paid or freely volunteered work, written records, and resources the community actually has. A job left for 'someone' is an unassigned job; this is why Greg's Starwake Pier fundraiser needs a real volunteer sheet, not the assumption that the performer will work every task. The fundraiser pays for railings first; the storage shed is not automatically funded.

## 4. Personhood and prior loyalties

Danse remains responsible for the beliefs and harm of his Brotherhood past. **Haylen's letter** comes through an indirect trader and explicitly forbids an unsafe reply on an old frequency. Danse writes an unsent response and waits for consensual, secure contact; nothing in the magic system lets him spy on her, deliver messages against her will or endanger the settlement.

**Ash** is a new visitor from the Commonwealth whose identity is private. Danse's old faction habits do not grant him authority over Ash. Repair work, a safe room and a secure door are immediate needs; there is no implied duty to forgive him. Ash's willingness to return for another repair is not absolution.

## 5. Archives are not confessional stages

Moonroot Archive may preserve records of war and dangerous historical science without forcing anyone to become public entertainment. The false recipe annotation is preserved as evidence, while a safe corrected copy is distributed. Iroh's Ba Sing Se siege map is cataloged with honest attribution but not put on public display until people affected by the siege can participate in contextualizing it. A war criminal's regret alone is not the public's complete history. Severus may refuse Iroh's tea invitation without forbidding Jasper from attending; care is not measured in forced attendance.

## 6. Romance continuity: ENM is specific, not collective entitlement

Jasper has already disclosed being **ethically non-monogamous** and preferring **polyamory** before these chapters. The three-person Avatar relationship with Jasper is not a single partner who can answer for everyone. **Katara's** Southern Water Tribe emergency temporarily takes her away; her relationship and needs continue during the absence. **Toph** and **Aang** maintain their own boundaries and obligations. Calendars distinguish one-on-one dates, shared household time, work, and alone time. A missed date matters because of lost notice, not because another love is automatically competition.

Greg's song does not secretly promise exclusivity. Steven may ask about his father's life without being asked to approve it. Maui's willingness to stay out of a traveler's departure is an act of respect, not a heroic conquest. Iroh can share the history of his military harm while being loved without receiving amnesty. Severus can decline a social gathering while remaining committed to Jasper.

**Sensuality guidance:** Keep tenderness specific to the participants—warm hands on a workbench, a kiss after an honest disagreement, the smell of sea air and fabric, a gentle private night—without graphic sex. Later private scenes should fade to black only when dramatically earned; not every chapter needs one.

- Maui's chapters 14 onward explore the tide-resistant garden, departure rights, the canal and old records without rushing to solve the Closed Bells mystery.
- Severus's next chapter revisits the old war letters; Iroh's map sits in an archive that can learn to display historical testimony responsibly.
- Riverstone's bridge closure and Katara's absence affect the work schedule and provisions, but neither can be repaired by wishing.
- Danse's encounter with Ash and Haylen's letter will inform safe communication practices and protective measures at the Threadways.
