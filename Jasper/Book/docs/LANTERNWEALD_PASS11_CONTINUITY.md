# Lanternweald — Eleventh-Pass Continuity and Interconnected Living World

**Established from on-page events on November 5, 2026.** This supplements, rather than replaces, the earlier world bibles. Six linear novels share a seven-district valley and voluntary Threadways to separate homeworlds. The valley is an original story setting, not a Dreamlight Valley reproduction: its comparable appeal comes from recurring residents, meaningful routines, earned repairs, public celebrations, everyday discoveries and overlapping character histories.

## What became canon on the page

### The Shore I Choose — chapters 33, 34, 37, 45

At **Starwake Pier**, Greg has the right to leave a song unfinished, and Maui learns that being asked to sit and listen is not a lesser form of friendship. Tamsin can ask for a new inspection even after an earlier stage check passed; a change in wind is a new condition, not proof that the prior inspector lied. Steven joins the group without a right to hear an unfinished private song.

At **Brasswatch and Tideglass**, the corroding salvaged fastener demonstrates why a component rated for dry work cannot be treated as suitable for coastal spray. The failed lot is quarantined, replacement specifications recorded, and the public sign distinguishes accessible detours, limited loads and safe tides. Danse, Tamsin, Fen, Lio, Renn and Maui have different roles; no single famous person's approval is final.

In the **Vel housing dispute**, the safe hall room is an available, independently administered alternative to Maui volunteering Jasper's shared living space. A shelter is not a reward for forgiving anyone. The housing registrar keeps private information private, and a guest may choose who escorts them. Maui cannot demand a conversation as payment for an apology.

At the **repair celebration**, Maui declines a hero-centered performance. Pip's event committee corrects the schedule, lets Greg choose his set length, pays for supplies, preserves clear access routes, and thanks private volunteers without public identification. A festival needs preparation, cleanup, wages and neighbor approval, not just lanterns and applause.

### After Always — chapters 31–32

At **Spinner's End**, Severus considers whether to keep a damaged house without pretending that a repair charm fixes structural, airflow or egress problems. A qualified builder may be paid to inspect it, but Severus is not obligated to convert the home into a public laboratory or training center. The house is in his homeworld, not relocated to Moonroot.

Lily's memory is neither a saintly prop nor an obstacle Jasper must defeat. Jasper distinguishes their non-exclusive, consensual commitments from the right to demand someone else's full history. The conversation is intimate, imperfect and continuing; mutual affection cannot erase the war, the house or Lily's independent personhood.

### Jasmine After Rain — chapters 22, 24, 26, 28, 34

These five chapters take place primarily in **Ba Sing Se**, at the Jasmine Dragon and its nearby market—not in the valley's Jasmine Steps. Mina is a paid employee with the right to close on time, organize safe aisles and refuse unpaid overtime. The familiar chair becomes usable rather than permanently reserved; an old history can be honored without obstructing work.

Iroh is not always a guru. He can have a sweet tooth, sing poorly, misjudge his schedule, want to be missed, lose a Pai Sho game and accept a genuinely limited invitation. He agrees not to volunteer Jasper's hours or Mina's labor in the name of kindness. White Lotus contact Ren asks rather than assumes; a non-emergency meeting may wait until morning.

The **lantern-market evening** includes the crooked turtleduck lantern, a posted price, awkward dancing and mutually chosen public affection. Jasper's openness about ethical non-monogamy and preference for polyamory is not permission for a market crowd to interrogate the partners. The turtleduck returns to the shop and is physically kept away from the stove by Mina.

### Love & Duty — chapters 43–44

Katara's **Southern Water Tribe clinic** has local practitioners, their own treatment decisions and requested training materials. Her journey supports that staff without implying they cannot continue without her. Aang can provide transport after asking, and Toph may remain at Riverstone rather than participate in every departure. A shared romantic household can have four honest needs rather than one collective schedule.

At **Riverstone Terrace**, aerial observation reveals a plausible runoff route but cannot substitute for Sela's dated water measurements. Sela retains control of her original record book. A gardener's seed trays can stop a proposed shortcut. The historic underground flood barrier remains sealed.

### What the Armor Leaves Behind — chapters 42–43

Haylen's letter is **private and unsent in reply** until a safe channel is agreed. The packet of Commonwealth components must be tested like other salvaged stock, even if it comes from a trusted person. No public notice should identify a protected correspondence without permission.

At **Brasswatch**, Ash's temporary accommodation is independent of labor. Working a shift is paid, voluntarily accepted and reviewed by the civilian complaints process. Danse's old military command style does not override the agency of Ash, Renn, Tamsin or Jasper. These are practical consequences, not automatic absolution for Brotherhood history.

### Stars Between the Strings — chapters 22, 27, 29, 33

These scenes take place in **Beach City and its neighboring homeworld locations**. Greg's local fundraiser gives him a choice of songs, safe sound equipment and an actual agreement with an organizer. A missed dinner leads to a specific communication repair rather than flowers that buy forgiveness. A weekend festival trip has real travel time, a budget, non-assumed performance commitments and a right to leave early.

Greg's storage room contains old posters, an old family letter, a damaged suitcase and an electrical hazard. Keeping or storing difficult belongings is his decision, with work held until a qualified electrician can inspect the wiring. His past is neither discarded nor given authority over the present. The community-center funding dispute and the job-distance offer in neighboring chapters remain unresolved by these events.

## The valley has a year, not just a festival backdrop

**Spring / thaw:** Riverstone gardens plan seedlings while Fen watches surface channels for debris and ice damage. The square's notice board may change daily. Workers need to inspect rather than assume the first sunny day proves the ground is safe.

**Summer / high trade:** Jasmine Steps manages tea deliveries and fresh water; Tideglass checks salt-corrosion ratings; Starwake plans performances around wind, heat, equipment safety and paid setup. Visits from homeworlds remain invitations, never summons.

**Autumn / preparation:** Mara tallies stores, Tamsin schedules lantern-hook checks, Neri prepares a public emergency-route map with a separate private contact registry, and gardeners record whose land receives runoff. The repair celebration and harvest dinners have different organizers and no automatic celebrity centerpiece.

**Winter / Frostturn:** Keeping a warm, accessible route matters more than a dramatic procession. Quiet grief rituals remain optional. Tea, blankets, staff time and safe crossings require money, inventory and the consent of the people supplying them. Homeworld calendars should not magically synchronize with the valley's local seasons.

## Original history: evidence, not instant revelations

The **Closed Bells** episode left incomplete civil records and a sealed historic lower service passage. The seventh scratch can be studied through lawful inspection, but it is not a portal, an unexplored dungeon open for adventure or proof of the erased keeper's identity. Moonroot's imperfect records, Fen's newer surface-flow readings and Tamsin's repair reports may contradict one another honestly. New evidence can challenge an interpretation; it cannot retroactively erase suffering or grant the investigators a right to trespass.

The **three civic layers** should be legible whenever they recur:
1. *Public*: service availability, posted closures, certified repair limits, published expenditures and optional festival credits.
2. *Restricted*: emergency contacts, private testimony, voluntary travel details and witness identities accessed only through an accountable process.
3. *Personal*: friendships, romance, grief, letters and household decisions that no council or love interest owns.

## Future cross-novel possibilities — NOT EVENTS ALREADY WRITTEN

A seasonal tool-lending fair could send one verified Brasswatch fastener to a Tideglass repair and a Moonroot receipt to the public archive; a misplaced receipt might require an apology rather than unveil an ancient villain. A Starwake rehearsal could be rescheduled after Fen posts wind warnings, leaving Greg free to decline and Maui free to attend just to listen. Mina and Mara could compare bakery and teahouse accounting without merging the Jasmine Dragon into Lanternweald. Katara's clinic could request supplies while Sela continues to control Riverstone water decisions. Severus might teach archival uncertainty instead of offering historical redemption. Danse could demonstrate a new safety rule with a civilian trainee who is not required to admire him.

Each possible scene needs dates, limits, who makes the decision, what changes afterward, and how the same decision appears (or doesn't) in another book. They are **editorial seeds only**, not new chapter plot points.

## Romance and voice guardrails

Jasper is an adult, first-person named reader-insert who directly discloses ethical non-monogamy and a preference for polyamory to partners. Consent is ongoing; a warm kiss does not authorize future intimacy. Partners have independent partners, schedules, obligations, fears and refusals. Flirting may be awkward, funny or shy; the prose may linger on sensory closeness before a natural fade-to-black, never explicit description. An apology must produce a changed behavior, not an instant reset. Use the corpus-level guidance in `json/william-writing-style.json`; do not copy source sentences verbatim.

**Continuity invariant:** six *completed* 50-chapter linear novels; no seventh compulsory questline, no forced group romance, no altered endings, and no guarantee that all chapters now have equal depth.
