## Actual editorial scenes

### Iroh: strategy without becoming the narrator's therapist

**Chapter 6**, *A Game of Pai Sho*: A young visitor presents a broken tile allegedly made by a White Lotus master. Iroh distinguishes a useful observation from unverifiable provenance, then leaves the choice of carpenter to the owner. His former strategic certainty is active in conversation, not turned into a generic parable.

**Chapter 19**, *A Room Above the Tea Shop*: Jasper's separate door key and independent creative work are acknowledged without making a room over the shop a standing employment agreement. Desire has a domestic, physical expression; Iroh accepts boundaries without turning the moment into an abstract speech.

**Chapter 36**, *A Promise with No Audience*: Jasper explicitly states ethical non-monogamy and preference for polyamory. Iroh is allowed jealousy about work and archives shared with Severus; he can ask for Friday rather than treating love as a contest he must win. This scene links Moonroot to Jasmine Steps with distinct points of view.

### Aang, Katara, and Toph: culture, material safety and unequal labor

**Chapter 6**, *What the Ground Knows*: Toph helps inspect the actual dangerous hoist and frayed rope behind Jasper's encounter with a falling crate. Self-defense remains important, but workers and the next shift must also be protected.

**Chapter 19**, *The Wrong Kind of Silence*: Jasper's new Air Nomad school form accidentally gives the Avatar responsibility for every maintenance request. Aang and the staff divide paid building oversight from actual teaching. The narrator's exhaustion does not evaporate at the moment a delegation plan succeeds.

**Chapter 36**, *A Future We Choose*: Katara challenges a mutual-aid application asking for Air Nomad genealogy. The eligibility restriction is removed, family records are protected, and Aang's school remains a living culture rather than an inheritance test or prophecy about who will bear his children.

### Danse: protection, vulnerability, and someone else's expertise

**Chapter 6**, *Steel and Scrap*: Haylen's transmitter expertise and pickup-window reality supersede Danse's urge to command another aircraft. Rhys's humor remains different from Haylen's frustration, and Jasper notices a soldier beginning to ask.

**Chapter 19**, *Under the Tanks*: A Prydwen radstorm includes real maintenance sounds, scheduled reports and uncertainty. Danse cannot make fear irrational by issuing a reassurance. Non-explicit intimacy emerges when two adults admit they want company.

**Chapter 36**, *Three Chords*: Greg visits with strings and checks the instrument before blaming Danse's hand. Greg's colloquial music-gear humor and Danse's stiff procedural style appear *in the same scene*; neither becomes a second Iroh. The music problem has both human and mechanical causes.

### Severus: technical competence, guarded attraction, and duty

**Chapter 6**, *A Question of Antidotes*: Snape's ledger retains failed attempts and unverified reagent observations rather than claiming certainty. His sharp safety instruction protects Jasper without turning the student–teacher dynamic into a romance template.

**Chapter 19**, *The Apology He Hates*: Flirtation is quills, annotations, argumentative breakfast and a tentative hand across a table. A threatening prison letter remains an unresolved pressure, not solved by being kissed.

**Chapter 36**, *A Better Kind of Classroom*: Minerva insists that returning students must be able to report unsafe teaching without confronting the teacher who frightened them. Severus contributes a real procedural improvement, not a theatrical apology. Professor Sinistra's on-page role as lesson observer is distinguished from her canonical position as Astronomy professor.

### Greg: unpolished musician, father, and lover

**Chapter 6**, *Do Not Make Me Guess*: Awkward morning-after teasing, coffee-machine frustration, borrowed clothing and mutually initiated kissing build heat without explicit description or manufactured philosophical speeches.

**Chapter 19**, *The Door Left Open*: The independent apartment viewing addresses Greg's jealousy of a place that will see Jasper more often than he does. Jasper explicitly restates ethical non-monogamy and preference for polyamory; Greg understands that it does not transfer possession of keys, shelves, home or his van. The moment ends in a physically clumsy kiss.

**Chapter 36**, *When We Choose Each Other*: A stranger's requested love-song dedication would erase Greg's life as a traveling musician and misread the relationship as exclusive rescue. He turns it down, retaining music, business, fatherhood and romantic choice as separate meaningful parts of life.

### Maui: generosity, fame, and the right to say no

**Chapter 6**, *A Song for the Unfinished*: Mereana's visiting group handles its own fragile instruments. Maui accepts crowd-control duty instead of commandeering their cases. Chapter 6 explicitly identifies Mereana as a Māori performer and teacher from Aotearoa.

**Chapter 19**, *The Apprenticeship of Asking*: Sixteen-year-old Lio acts as the boatbuilding apprentice, directing Maui in physically supporting a plank for Sela's commissioned skiff. Lio is a *minor supporting character*, not a romance route.

**Chapter 36**, *Permission for Privacy*: Public rumors about Jasper's partners cause real discomfort. Mara defends the bakery boundary, while Maui holds a bucket rather than turning his outrage into a public spectacle that would override Jasper's own voice.

## Editorial limits

This pass substantively revised 18 of 300 existing chapters. It did not rewrite every chapter or equalize all six voices by imposing a universal paragraph count. Chapter-count parity is true; equal literary depth across every chapter remains an editorial goal, not a verified result.

The controlling style reference remains `json/william-writing-style.json`: first-person emotional immediacy, sensory and ordinary-world anchors, specific action, dialogue with consequence and humor that belongs to its speaker. Each partner must retain the ability to refuse or renegotiate intimacy. Sensual scenes remain adult, non-explicit and private, with fade-to-black where appropriate. Avoid repeating the same apology, “can I kiss you” sequence or final-lesson paragraph in every novel.
