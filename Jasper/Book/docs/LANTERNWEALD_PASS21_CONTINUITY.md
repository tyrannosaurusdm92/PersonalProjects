## Shared-world rules

Lanternweald connects six linear fanfiction novels through physical destinations, voluntary Threadways, public services, supply chains, workshops, couriers, archives and actual neighbors. The connection does not erase the distinctive rules of Ba Sing Se, Cranefish Town, the Southern Water Tribe, the Commonwealth, Hogwarts and Beach City. Jasper's relationship to each love interest is ethical non-monogamy by explicit disclosure and a preference for polyamory, **not** an assumption that all partners become one household or share all private spaces.

The old **lower Closed Bells maintenance passage remains sealed**. A successful storm test of the **temporary upper surface channel** is not evidence that the dangerous historic passage is usable, nor proof that the new route is safe under every tide. The old erased keeper's identity remains unknown; Moonroot records do not gain certainty through a famous visitor's speculation.

## District and character intersections grounded in Pass 21 chapters

### Jasmine Steps and Ba Sing Se — shopkeeping, gardening and grief

- *Jasmine After Rain* **12:** Iroh's Fire Nation travel plan requires a genuine delegation agreement. Mina can accept or reject spoiled tea deliveries without losing wages. The letter to Zuko remains his own, not a shop order.
- **25:** Lian, the neighboring gardener, chooses planting and testing on her own side of the wall. Huan is a market grower recommended by Lian; no scene states Huan's age, pronouns or family ties.
- **47:** Halen chooses where to sit after bereavement. Iroh's history of losing Lu Ten informs his understanding, but does not entitle him to manage Halen's mourning.

**Iroh's voice:** A strategic former general with warm, deliberate humor, not a generalized soft-spoken sage. He can want to arrange everything and still accept the ordinary authority of Mina, Lian and Halen.

### Riverstone, Cranefish Town and the Southern Water Tribe — consent cannot be a press strategy

- *Love & Duty* **12:** Katara protects a patient's privacy from someone trying to manufacture a household scandal; Aang and Toph do not get to speak for her patients or for each other's boundaries.
- **25:** The shoreline ice has a real danger; Gran Gran, Hakoda, local families and the patrol decide where markers and routes belong. Aang's bending and Katara's healing do not replace the community's continuing responsibility.
- **47:** The Longlight Festival's kite design belongs to the apprentices who made it. Aang can help them repair it without converting the display into an Air Nomad sacred act or receiving credit he hasn't earned.

**Voices:** Aang is playful but must reckon with public responsibility and cultural survival. Katara asks who is affected and resists becoming indispensable at the cost of her own life. Toph challenges faulty physical assumptions and enjoys puncturing grandiosity, but must never replace specialists' testing with omniscience.

### Brasswatch Yard, Brotherhood and public repair rights

- *What the Armor Leaves Behind* **12:** A suspect aircraft coupling delays the supply run. Danse respects qualified pilot and technician responsibilities rather than making a uniform the final structural assessment.
- **25:** Haylen chooses the extent of her assistance in Jasper's search for Danse. She is allowed to refuse a dangerous escort and decline to make his homecoming another debt.
- **47:** A workshop complaint about a supervisor needs an independent reviewer, possible outside witness and privacy safeguards. Ash and Renn make the important correction, not Danse alone.

**Danse's voice:** Direct, risk-aware, frequently over-structured. He can make a disciplined tactical choice and still be wrong about whom he may command. Supporting workers are not props for his redemption.

### Spinner's End, Moonroot and Hogwarts historical evidence

- *After Always* **12:** Severus corrects a falsely damaging school record without requiring a former pupil to thank or forgive him.
- **25:** A peeling vial label creates physical closeness and dry, prickly dialogue. The burnt Prophet headline does not reappear in the next paragraph as if it had been kept whole.
- **47:** Orin's oral-history research must use opt-in witness contact and protect addresses in private correspondence. Severus can insist on correct provenance without dictating which witness must speak.

**Severus's voice:** Precise, sardonic, angry at bad evidence and often at himself. Affection can be a quiet practical exchange without sounding like either Iroh's strategic generosity or Greg's impromptu songs. History is not his personal redemption exhibition.

### Starwake Pier and Beach City — sound gear, vehicle repair and nonexclusive home

- *Stars Between the Strings* **12:** Jasper gives space to a photograph of young Steven without making the room or every shelf belong to Greg. Greg understands that a polyamorous relationship is not blanket access to housing.
- **25:** A broken car-wash pump has lost sales, customer refunds and a coherent technician sequence. Alva comes at two and inspects the pump; Greg asks her about future maintenance, then reports actual tested conditions rather than promising instant repairs.
- **47:** The fundraiser must pay the contracted sound/electrical technician instead of substituting a name on a program. The event specialist stays unnamed: Alva's observed role as **pump-repair technician** must not be misrepresented as event electrical certification.

**Greg's voice:** Dad-jokey, musically capable, occasionally avoidant and sometimes practically sharp. Cables, fuel, family photographs and awkward jokes belong in his world. Avoid Iroh's poised military metaphors.

### Lanternwell and Tideglass — voluntary departures and art ownership

- *The Shore I Choose* **12:** Lark asks for a confidential departure. Pip and Neri consider changing the return-date register, and Maui offers to help without demanding to know the route. Lark's destination and past remain private.
- **25:** Mereana arrives with visiting artists from Aotearoa and has real authority about art-panel handling, possible restoration, display rights and travel delays. Chapter 6 explicitly identifies Mereana as a Māori performer and teacher. Do not infer a particular iwi or ceremonial authority, or invent an unwritten tradition.
- **47:** Fen reports the tide, blockage and accessibility limits of the temporary upper channel. Maui's desire to do something impressive cannot make every tide safe or reopen the sealed historic passage.

**Maui's voice:** Physically daring, boastful, funny and acutely aware of audience expectations; Mini Maui can undercut the act. He learns to ask about consent and the engineering plan without turning into a generic mild-mannered romantic lead.
