# Character Research Audit

Research pass: September 19, 2026. The supplied first-person reader-insert
guide and route notes were used as project references. The separate reference
book was consulted only for the cover/open-book interaction; no poems, stories,
data, or JavaScript from it were imported. Episode summaries and
character-history references were checked before expanding the routes.

## Greg Universe

Applied findings: music is relationship language; Greg's humor can protect
vulnerability; Rose and Steven remain part of his history; the van, car wash,
Beach City, community, money, and road trips create external plot; Greg is
warm and capable without becoming an idiot or a generic horny dad; he asks
before touching and learns to receive care.

## Iroh

Applied findings: original-series Iroh is a retired general, powerful
firebender, strategist, and White Lotus leader; Lu Ten's death shapes his
patience and generosity; tea, food, Pai Sho, plants, music, and flirting are
embodied habits; his calm can become fierce when innocents are threatened; he
is wise but not omniscient, and the reader must care for him as a man.
