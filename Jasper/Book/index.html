Welcome to the Lanternweald

A magical vale where doorways lead to Ba Sing Se, Cranefish Town, the Commonwealth, the wizarding world, and Beach City. Jasper's journeys connect the neighborhoods without erasing anyone's own home or history.

Begin at Tideglass Cove with Maui, then visit Jasmine Steps, Riverstone Terrace, Brasswatch Yard, Moonroot Archive, and Starwake Pier. Each story still reads from its first chapter to its last; the Lanternweald interludes are extra scenes, never choices or alternate endings.
