# Fifteenth-Pass Voice Separation: Maui, Iroh, Greg and Friends

Applied to the **existing novels** on November 6, 2026. This is character-writing guidance, not new film canon or an assertion of a perfect reproduction of an individual writer's voice. See `../docs/PROSE_DEPTH_PASS15_AUDIT.md` for the nine edited chapter slots.

## When characters are kind, they must still sound different

**Maui (Moana film interpretation):** Opens with a gigantic claim. Improvises, flirts, competes, exaggerates, resents being overlooked and wants recognition. His hook and Disney-specific animated tattoos don't fix consent, public safety, budgets or another person's feelings. With Greg's song, Mereana's property, Neri's testimony and Pip's festival, his personality is loud even when he chooses not to take control. Moana remains a capable independent heroine, not someone Jasper replaces. Never treat screen Maui's fictional magic as traditional Māori cultural genealogy.

**Uncle Iroh (ATLA):** A tea-maker and former general with tactical reflexes, grief, pride and an occasional habit of taking on other people's schedules. He may play Pai Sho several moves ahead, draw a frighteningly practical map, and still need Mina to enforce her paid hours. Humor comes from appetite, gentle mischief, battle memory and not always wanting to discuss that memory. Avoid giving him generic counselor monologues.

**Greg Universe (Steven Universe):** A working musician and father with a van, a car wash, experience with unfinished songs and a knack for self-deprecating humor. He hums when words are hard; he may offer too much out of fear of being left. His actual voice can ramble and stumble, not sound like Iroh's polished strategy. He owns his own unfinished music.

**Severus Snape:** Dry, caustic, exacting and capable of wrong decisions under stress. Professional and ethical repair takes concrete evidence, not a grand confession. Pomfrey can contradict him.

**Paladin Danse:** Technical, procedural, protective and not naturally fluent in emotional reassurance. Let him evaluate pressure gauges and be irritated when a local mechanic is right. Loyalty is demonstrated through specific work, not another person's speech.

**Aang / Katara / Toph:** Aang tries hope and imaginative teaching, sometimes avoiding conflict. Katara catches vulnerable people's unmet needs and insists on practical care. Toph challenges sentiment with blunt humor, physical facts and independence. Their disagreements shouldn't resolve into three identical lectures.

## Crossover rule

A cameo must change something tangible without taking over the host story: a performance slot, a paid shift, a public testimony, an access route, a work order or an honest admission. Jasper remains an agent rather than an all-purpose interpreter for famous characters. A kind line that could be swapped between Maui, Greg and Iroh without anyone noticing needs another pass.

## Cultural boundary

The research in this `Maui/` folder differentiates Disney's Moana fiction from Māori and other Polynesian source traditions. Mereana's fictional modern ensemble decides which of its own work to perform, how it travels and how it is represented. A source file is not permission to fabricate iwi-specific ceremony or an invented sacred tattoo design.
