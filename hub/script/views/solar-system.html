Aza'ra System

Aza'ra is a ~27,060 km-wide flat circular world sealed beneath the 80% transparent Veil. Its four continents—Dakaroth, Arzuhan, Tzaphaer, and Belavadös—and its floating islands remain inside the membrane. The central stellar body is nearly dead and supplies no meaningful daylight. Aza'ra exists in perpetual twilight, lit by the Veil-powered sacred moons Duvaen, W’iosamn, and Arvalla.

The Veil shields the disc from Voidstorms and entropy, filters arcane flow and planar corruption, and acts as a dimensional membrane where deities, spirits, and forgotten thoughts can drift between realms. As it digests soul-magic siphoned through rift portals connected to Earth, its resonant hums define Aza'raan hours and minutes.
