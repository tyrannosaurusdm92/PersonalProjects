# Happy 63rd Birthday, Mom

Extract the ZIP and open **birthday.html**. Press **Begin**. No internet connection, login, or installation is needed.

The tribute uses the complete 177-photo collection, shuffled into a new order when playback begins or when **Watch again** is selected. The photographed family members are not named on screen.

### Music

1. Pachelbel — **Canon in D**
2. Beethoven — **Für Elise**
3. Debussy — **Clair de Lune**

These three supplied recordings are combined into one local soundtrack with a 4.5-second overlap into Für Elise and a 4.8-second overlap into Clair de Lune. Unwanted silence at the beginning of Canon and Für Elise was removed. The final recording lasts **13 minutes, 36.7 seconds**.

### Design

The motion design uses dancing waves, dust, and light curtains in amethyst, lilac, heather, iris, mauve, orchid, and other shades of purple. The flower illustration combines white roses, lilac flowers, and lavender sprigs. Their petals descend gradually over the photographs, which are kept uncropped.

### Controls

Play/Pause, backward/forward 10 seconds, seek, sound and fullscreen. Keyboard: **Space** or **K** to pause/play, **←** and **→** to seek, **M** to mute, **F** for fullscreen. Audio starts on the first user interaction due to browser audio permissions. Reduced-motion system settings are supported.

### Contents

`birthday.html` is the only HTML file. `js/`, `css/`, `json/`, and `docs/` are flat. The `assets/` folder contains `images/` and `audio/`. The photo deck and audio energy data are embedded in JS for reliable offline use when opening `birthday.html` directly from disk.

Music and family photographs are provided for this personal tribute; they are not licensed for public redistribution by this package.
