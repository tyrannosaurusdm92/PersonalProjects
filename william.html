AI-Brain is my reusable project-aware intelligence corpus. Gemini supplies model reasoning; AI-Brain supplies retrieval targets, routing/helper JavaScript, structured knowledge, reusable assets, and project-support patterns.

What the corpus currently covers

Conversation/planning/reasoning support; memory and project continuity; personas, agents and NPCs; creative writing, publishing, story continuity and conlangs; worldbuilding; games, TTRPGs, MMORPGs and VTT systems; maps, simulation and strategy; studio/art operations; 3D modeling and repair; software/web/mobile/PWA/auth/data-schema/debugging/testing; file classification/deduplication/version consolidation; organization/project management; social/community/safety/accessibility; and large general healthcare, psychiatry, mental-health, neurodevelopment, trauma, disability, life-event, injury, neurological and oncology knowledge collections.

Person-specific health facts remain separate from the public reusable corpus.
